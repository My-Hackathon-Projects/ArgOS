## Voiceover-only (for practicing)

1. Great founders are invisible until they know the right person. Their story is scattered across hackathons, papers and launches. Here is how to find them.
2. ArgOS is an Operating System for VC Funds
3. It starts with the investment thesis, you specify what industries, type of founders etc. you are interested in. 
4. ArgOS collects all founder signals that are relevant for you.
5. It also provides a searchable overview of founders that have been discovered and are now being tracked. In the detail view for each profile it calculates a founder score that is  continuously updated with new information. All the raw signals are used to derive evidence-based claims that power the scoring.
6. Under decisions, for each startup idea you see the 3-axis scoring that the underlying research agents collected evidence for. Those reports are updated each time a new claim about a monitored founder is generated. You can see the market, competitor analyses, KPI benchmarks and the investment memo. All of that with the relevant sources.
